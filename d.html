09 — Enquiries

Frequently asked.

Does the exchange trade, or hold client assets?

No. Alpha Exchange is a marketplace. It never routes an order, never manages a portfolio and never takes custody of client assets. Systems are licensed to the buyer and deployed on the buyer's own infrastructure, under the buyer's own regulatory permissions.

How are track records verified?

Every listing is reconciled against broker statements and independently attested. Members may replicate the record from the raw trade log inside the data room before any commitment is made. A record that cannot be replicated is withdrawn and the vendor is removed.

What exactly is delivered on settlement?

The licence, the complete source code, the trained model weights, the deployment documentation and the capacity study — released from tri-party escrow against payment in USD. Nothing is delivered as a compiled black box.

Are prices negotiable?

Licence fees are fixed by the vendor and published in the catalogue. They do not float and are not subject to bidding. Exclusive and pre-listing mandates are priced by arrangement with the desk.

Why is capacity stated on every listing?

Because it is the honest limit of a strategy. Beyond stated capacity, market impact degrades the edge. Vendors who overstate capacity are delisted; the figure is re-verified every ninety days.

Can a system be resold?

Licences are non-transferable. A member wishing to exit may surrender the licence to the vendor, who may re-list it. Exclusive mandates carry a right of first refusal in favour of the exchange.
